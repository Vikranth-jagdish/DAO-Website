# DAO Community Website

Welcome to the official website of DAO Community, VIT Chennai: [daocommunity.in](https://daocommunity.in)

## About DAO Community

DAO Community is a vibrant student-led club at VIT Chennai, dedicated to fostering innovation, collaboration, and learning in the fields of blockchain, web3, and decentralized technologies. Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, hands-on experience, and networking opportunities in the rapidly evolving world of decentralized systems.

### What We Do
- Organize workshops, hackathons, and seminars on blockchain and web3.
- Host events featuring industry experts and thought leaders.
- Build a supportive community for students interested in emerging technologies.
- Provide resources and mentorship for project development and research.

## About the Website

The DAO Community website serves as a central hub for:
- Event announcements and registrations
- Gallery of past and upcoming events
- Information about our sponsors and partners
- Details on student benefits and club initiatives
- Contact and social media links
